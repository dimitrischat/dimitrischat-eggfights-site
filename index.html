Whose egg survives?

The Easter egg-tapping tradition, rebuilt as a mobile game.

Every Easter, people knock two dyed eggs together and the one that does not crack wins. In Greece it is called tsougrisma, in Serbia tucanje jajima, in Romania ciocnitul ouălor. EggFights turns that moment into a game you can play any day of the year.

Get it on Google Play
Free, for Android. Available now in Serbia and Romania. Coming to Greece for Easter.

What you do

  • Collect eggs. Every egg has its own strength, weight and shape — and those three things decide who wins.
  • Duel a friend next to you. Local multiplayer connects two phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No accounts, no internet needed.
  • Play the campaign. Work through opponents on your own and build up a basket worth fighting with.
  • Side games. EggRun, where you balance an egg on a spoon, and EggCatcher, where you catch falling eggs.

About

EggFights is made in Greece. No banner ads, no forced interstitials, and no paywall — just optional rewarded videos if you want a boost. Questions: support@eggfights.com
