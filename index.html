Whose egg survives?

A Greek Easter tradition, rebuilt as a mobile game.

Every Greek Easter, people knock two dyed eggs together and the one that does not crack wins. It is called tsougrisma. EggFights turns that moment into a game you can play any day of the year.

What you do

  • Collect eggs. Every egg has its own strength, weight and shape — and those three things decide who wins.
  • Duel a friend next to you. Local multiplayer connects two phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. No accounts, no internet lobby.
  • Play the campaign. Work through opponents on your own and build up a basket worth fighting with.
  • Run the gauntlet. EggRun is a side game where you balance an egg on a spoon and try not to drop it.
