Support

We read every message.

Email: support@eggfights.com
Typical response time: 2–3 business days.

Before you write

Including these details helps us solve your problem much faster:

  • Your phone model and Android version
  • Your in-game player name
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or short screen recording, if possible

Common questions

Local multiplayer will not connect

Both phones need Bluetooth and Wi-Fi switched on, must be within a few metres of each other, and must have granted the permissions the game requests on first launch. Both players should be on the multiplayer screen at the same time. If it still fails, close and reopen the game on both devices.

I lost my progress

Progress is tied to an anonymous account on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can remove that link permanently. If you think progress was lost for another reason, email us with your player name and roughly when you last played.

The rewarded ad did not give me my reward

The reward is granted when the video finishes. If the ad was closed early, interrupted by a call, or lost connection, the reward may not register. If a completed ad did not pay out, email us with the approximate time it happened.

No ads are available

Ad availability depends on third-party ad networks and varies by region and time of day. Trying again later usually helps. This is not a bug in the game.

How do I change my ads consent choice?

Open the in-game settings and reopen the privacy options. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling removes local data. To delete data stored on our servers, email support@eggfights.com with your in-game player ID. See section 10 of the Privacy Policy.

Bug reports and ideas

EggFights is made by one person. Bug reports and suggestions genuinely shape what gets built next — send them to support@eggfights.com.
